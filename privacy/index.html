Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Armory AI ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Armory AI mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what the App needs to work:

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored only as a one-way hash). There is no social sign-in.
  • Catalog content you add: item details (manufacturer, model, caliber, serial number, condition, purchase date and price, notes, tags), photos, documents and receipts, and range logs.
  • App settings and choices, such as reminder preferences and whether you allow AI identification.
  • Subscription status: whether you have Armory AI Pro. Payments are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment details.
  • If you turn on reminders, a push notification token for your device.
  • If you use Refer a Friend, a record of who referred whom.
  • Technical information: crash and error reports, and your IP address while you use the App, used for security such as limiting repeated sign-in attempts.

Location and device details are removed from every photo before it is stored or sent anywhere.

2. How We Use Information

  • To run the App: store your catalog and show it on your devices.
  • To identify items from photos, only if you allow AI identification (see section 3).
  • To check and improve the accuracy of AI identification.
  • To send account emails, such as password reset codes.
  • To send the reminders you turn on (cleaning, low ammo, yearly check-up).
  • To provide Armory AI Pro and referral rewards.
  • To keep the App secure and fix problems.

We do not show ads, we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we never sell your information.

3. AI Identification and Your Choice

The App can suggest an item's make, model, type, caliber, serial number, and condition from its photos. To do this, the photos you take of the item are sent to a third-party AI service run by another company. You check every suggestion before anything is saved.

  • AI identification is off until you allow it. We ask once, and your answer is saved to your account, so it applies on all your devices and after reinstalling the App.
  • Only the photos are sent. Your name, email address, account details, and the rest of your catalog are not sent.
  • Photos are used only to identify the item. They are never used for advertising and never sold.
  • You can turn AI identification off at any time in Settings → Identify with AI or on the AI Privacy screen. This withdraws your permission, and no new photos are sent.
  • Batch photo import uses the same AI service and the same permission.
  • The whole App works without AI: you can type in every detail yourself.

We keep a record of what the AI suggested and what you saved, linked to your account, to check and improve accuracy. Serial numbers are removed from these records.

4. Who We Share Information With

We share information only with service providers that help us run the App, and each receives only what it needs:

  • A third-party AI service: the photos you choose to have identified, only with your permission (see section 3).
  • Our hosting and file storage providers: your account and catalog data, so the App can store and show it.
  • A subscription-management service: your subscription status, not your payment details.
  • A crash-reporting service: error reports with your account ID, but not your email address. Serial numbers are removed from reports.
  • An email provider: your email address, only to deliver account emails.
  • Apple's push notification service: your device token, only if you turn on reminders. Notifications never include serial numbers.

Every service provider that receives your information is required to protect it at least as well as this Privacy Policy does, and to use it only to provide its service to us.

The App may also let you export or share your catalog (such as PDF or spreadsheet exports). Any sharing or export is done at your sole discretion and risk. We are not responsible for how exported or shared information is used, stored, or distributed by others.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law.

8. How Long We Keep Data and How to Delete It

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and its catalog, photos, documents, imports, and AI records.

After deletion, we may keep an anonymous count of AI usage for cost tracking, which is not linked to you. Deleted data may remain in routine backups for a limited time until those backups are replaced. To ask about your data or request deletion, email support@armoryai.app.

9. Your Choices

  • Turn AI identification on or off: Settings → Identify with AI.
  • Turn push notifications on or off: Settings → Push notifications.
  • Lock the App with Face ID or a PIN: Settings → App Lock.
  • Delete your account and data: Settings → Delete Account.
