October 2026

October 2026 in Armory AI

A big month: accounts, insurance claim packets, reminders, iPad support, and a new look.

  • Insurance claim packets
  • Maintenance and ammo reminders
  • Separate catalogs for gun trusts
  • iPad support
  • Choose what goes on an export
  • Email accounts and password reset
Read the full updateHide details

New

  • Create an account with your email, and reset your password with a code if you forget it.
  • Report a Loss builds a claim packet from your records, ready to send to your insurer.
  • Reminders for cleaning, low ammo and yearly check-ups, with optional notifications you can turn on or off one by one.
  • Multiple catalogs, for example to keep items held in a gun trust separate.
  • iPad support, including landscape.
  • Batch import now works for accessories and ammunition too.
  • Export and share your catalog with Armory AI Pro.
  • Choose what goes on an export: show serial numbers in full, only the last 4, or not at all, and leave out values, purchase dates, notes or photos.
  • Duplicate check: the app warns you when you save an item that's already in your catalog, and can scan your whole catalog from Settings.
  • Favorites, tags, your own sort order, quick actions and a range-trip log.
  • Collection stats, including what you've spent over time.
  • Achievements and badges, which you can share.
  • Share cards for your collection, with a choice of looks and highlight photos.
  • Dictate your notes instead of typing them.
  • AI identification recognizes accessory brands from their logos and designs.
  • Add an item by typing in its details, with no photo needed.
  • Refer a Friend.
  • Delete your account and all of its data from inside the app.
  • Choose an app icon, with light, dark and tinted versions.

Improved

  • You're asked once before any photo is sent for AI identification, and your answer follows you to every device.
  • Serial numbers are encrypted when stored.
  • A new Midnight and Brass look.
  • Photos and lists load faster.
  • Import groups photos into items more accurately, and you can swipe through photos.
  • Your collection refreshes when you come back to the app.
  • Prices show with a dollar sign.
  • Your profile picture is saved to your account, so it shows on every device.

Fixed

  • Face ID no longer locks the app again while it's unlocking.
  • Sharing your collection no longer hangs.
  • The range log no longer freezes after 50 rounds.
  • Camera controls no longer hide behind the preview.
  • Easier-to-read labels in light mode.
  • Security and reliability improvements.
September 2026

September 2026 in Armory AI

Adding a whole collection got much faster, with batch import and several photos per item.

  • Batch import from your photo library
  • Several photos per item
  • Faster AI identification
Read the full updateHide details

New

  • Batch import: pick a set of photos and Armory AI sorts them into items, so you can review each one before it's saved.
  • Take several photos of one item, choose its cover photo, and move a photo to a different item.
  • AI identification can look at several photos of an item at once for a better match.
  • Add photos straight from your photo library, even when your collection is empty.

Improved

  • AI identification is faster.
  • Uploads that get stuck now recover on their own.

Fixed

  • Undoing a photo change puts your photos back in the right order.
  • Photo and document uploads work reliably again.
  • Security and reliability improvements.
