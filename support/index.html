I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your account email. We'll email you a 6-digit code. Enter it with a new password and you're signed back in. Codes expire after 15 minutes.

I don't get the reset email

Check your spam or junk folder for a message from noreply@armoryai.app. If your account was created with a username before we added email sign-in, add an email first under Settings → Account → Email while you're signed in.

How do I export my catalog?

Go to Settings → Data → Export Catalog.

Something was lost or stolen

Go to Settings → Data → Report a Loss to build a claim packet with photos, serial numbers and values for your insurer or a police report.

How do I lock the app?

Turn on Settings → Security → App Lock to require Face ID, fingerprint or a PIN when Armory AI opens.

How do I delete my account?

In Armory AI, go to Settings → Delete Account and confirm with your password. Your account is deleted right away. Export anything you want to keep first.

No longer have the app? Email support@armoryai.app from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

Deleting your account permanently removes your account, catalog, serial numbers, photos, documents, range logs and AI records. We keep only an anonymous count of AI usage that isn't linked to you. Deleted data may stay in routine backups for a limited time until they’re replaced.

Want to keep your account but remove some data? Delete any item, photo or document in the app at any time, or turn off AI identification and price sharing in Settings. You can also email support@armoryai.app to ask us to delete specific data; we'll do it within 30 days.
